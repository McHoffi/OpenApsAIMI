### The layout trap

`mtr` still uses the pre-flip layout (`src/main`, `src/test`, `R.string`,
`titleResId: Int`). This fork lives at `androidMain` / `commonMain` /
`androidHostTest` and its `PreferenceKey` takes `title: TextRef` /
`summary: TextRef?`. Every mtr key and settings file had to be **ported**, not
copied:

- `BooleanKey.kt`, `DoubleKey.kt` and `ApsIntentKey.kt` came in at
  `src/main` (DU). Their new entries were ported into the `commonMain` copies
  with `KeysStrings` / `ApsStrings` and `TextRef`, then the `src/main` copies
  were `git rm`-ed.
- The 12 UA files (audit, manifest, screens, safety rules and their tests) were
  placed at the post-flip paths and adapted:
  `AimiSettingsAuditRow.title` is `TextRef?` (empty title becomes `null`);
  `AimiSettingsAuditChangedBy.label` is `TextRef`;
  `AimiValueDescriptor.value` is `TextRef?` (it already was);
  the screen imports `app.aaps.core.ui.compose.stringResource` and
  `ApsStrings` / `CoreUiStrings` — the Android `@StringRes` overload cannot sit
  next to the `TextRef` one (name clash).
- `ClaudeModelResolver.kt` was `git mv`-ed from `src/main` to `androidMain`.

`PreferenceSubScreenDef` keeps both constructors (`title: TextRef` and
`titleResId: Int` → `TextRef.AndroidRes`), so the many `titleResId = R.string.x`
call sites in `OpenAPSAIMIPlugin` still compile as they are.

### What arrived

- **Glucose circle home screen widget** (Glance): `GlucoseCircleWidget` and
  `WidgetConfigureActivity`, registered in `ui/src/androidMain/AndroidManifest.xml`
  next to the four widgets already there.
- **Autosens store work**: the published store is volatile; the newest bucket is
  pinned so it cannot lag the newest reading; the grid anchor is seeded from the
  phase the readings share and snapped in the right direction before the anchor;
  `bgReadings` and the window carbs are read once per pass, not once per element
  or per bucket; the bucketing mode flag survives `clone()`.
- **Extend an active loop suspend or pump disconnect** (`a46ba91a8b`): the same
  temporary mode is allowed again from `allowedNextModes()`. Picking a duration
  starts a new row from now, and `endReplacedTemporaryMode()` ends the old row
  after the insert, so the reconciler keeps the zero TBR and history has no
  overlap.
- **Retry a missing zero TBR** while the pump is disconnected, and alarm
  (`ZERO_DELIVERY_NOT_SET`).
- **Wear scenes and Loop Status cards**: end a scene with its follow-up, or skip
  to it, from the watch; the scene stop confirm says what ends before what
  starts; profile card and sensitivity card in Loop Status; a running mode the
  scene set is marked in the picker and in Loop Status.
- **SensitivityOverview extraction**: `buildSensitivityUiState()` moved out of
  `ChipsViewModel` into `SensitivityOverviewImpl` so the chip, its dialog and the
  watch share one set of rules. Upstream's implementation already carries this
  fork's behaviour (the hide-AS-100% rules, `getSensitivityOverviewString()`,
  `getIsfMgdlForCarbs`, `BooleanNonKey.AutosensUsedOnMainPhone`).
- **Dash `BolusType` plumbing**: `BolusType` moves to common, gains
  `BASAL_CORRECTION`, and the round-trip with `BS.Type` is made lossless. A
  `BASAL_CORRECTION` has no `BS.Type` and is never synced as one.
- **Dana RS emulator**: a switch that makes the pump refuse temp basals
  (`EMULATE_DANA_RS_REJECT_TBR`).
- **Carelevo**: dead alarm screen strings removed with CDATA cleanup, and
  placeholders numbered in strings with more than one.
- Dependabot bumps: metro 1.4.5, composeMultiplatform 1.12.1, wearCompose 1.7.0,
  androidx core-ktx 1.19.1, work 2.12.0, mockito 5.24.0, mockito-kotlin 6.4.0,
  gradle-wrapper 9.8.0. Plus Crowdin.

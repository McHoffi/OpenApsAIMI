## What arrived from upstream

- **Bucketing.** The grid moves when the newest readings have left it, so the loop no longer runs on
  a BG up to 4.5 minutes old (#5148, temporary).
- **Scenes.** A profile write a scene causes stays silent whichever change event leads to it
  (#4959), and a plainer text when its profile cannot be undone. After an insulin change during a
  scene, ending it ends its percentage too, and new profile switches take the insulin just changed
  (#5081). The scene wizard's step counter no longer ends on Step 9 of 8. New notification
  `SCENE_PROFILE_NOT_RESTORED` (appended at the end of `NotificationId`, see below).
- **Profile.** A switch that is no longer in force when the pump has set it gets no effective
  profile switch, so an ended scene or an old insulin does not come back for the next write. An
  insulin change during a temporary profile switch writes the new insulin to the permanent switch
  too (#5081).
- **Drawer.** Back closes it instead of leaving the app, and a screen opens only after it has closed.
- **Overview.** The COB icon keeps blinking in portrait while carbs are required.
- **Dialogs.** Secondary numbers are folded, and the wizard profile row opens its list in one tap.
- **Insulin / bolus.** The bolus progress dialog is updated. Dash bolus cancel retry delay fixed.
- **Medtronic.** A cancel or resume that a read brings late ends the entry before it, not the newest
  one (#3755). After a failed tune-up the driver tunes again by itself every 10 minutes (#3253).
- **Insight / pump pairing.** Insight pairing turns Bluetooth on when it is off, not off. Pump
  pairing waits for the Bluetooth check, so a scan without permission does not crash the app.
- **Search.** The wiki is searched in the app language too, not only in English.
- **Wear.** A tile with more than four actions shows the first four, not nothing. The wizard page
  shows the master's warnings, so a capped dose says so. Custom watchface ambient complication
  sources are hidden from the chooser.
- **Objectives.** Wiki links use anchors that are the same in every language; the emergency
  equipment link uses the new fixed FAQ label.
- **Version**: `4.0.0-dev-e`.

## What arrived from mtr

- **Dynamic basal stack cap.** No up-multiplier (held at x1.0) when IOB x ISF >= BG - 70, never
  during a declared meal. New key `OApsAIMIDynamicBasalStackCap` (`key_aimi_dynamic_basal_stack_cap`,
  ON by default). Exported in `dynamic_basal_stack_cap`. New `DynamicBasalStackCap` + test.
- **Forced early-meal TBR with a real predicted delta.** The meal-onset detector gets a real
  predicted rise instead of the same 5-min delta twice, so one noisy step cannot start it alone.
  Undeclared meals only. New key `OApsAIMIForcedTbrRealPredDelta`
  (`key_aimi_forced_tbr_real_pred_delta`, ON by default).
- **Declared meal tracking** gained a keyword, and `maxBaselineMaeRatio` was renamed to
  `maxBaselineErrorRatio`.

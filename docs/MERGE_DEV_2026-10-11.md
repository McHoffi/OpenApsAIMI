## What arrived from upstream

- **Notifications.** Alarms vibrate with per-channel patterns and readable channel names (#5217).
  A tap on a notification opens the running app again (`NotificationHolder.openAppIntent`)
  instead of rebuilding the task from `TaskStackBuilder` (#5221 follow-up).
- **Status lights and widgets refresh only while a screen shows them** (#5246).
  `StatusViewModel` collects `uiState.subscriptionCount` and only then runs its triggers.
  Pump status events are gone as a trigger: reservoir and battery StateFlows emit only on a
  real change.
- **Overview graphs use a visible-time-range window** (#5245). The Y scale and the chart extras
  look at the visible window, not the whole series.
- **Insulin U20 concentration** (`ConcentrationType.U20`).
- **QuickLaunch** hides Fill and battery change on patch pumps and inactive QuickWizard entries.
  `ElementAvailability` gates `FILL` and `BATTERY_CHANGE` on the pump capabilities.
- **Equil.** Low insulin and low battery alarm once per level; a reservoir change asks whether a
  new infusion set was put on; no 22 s wait for a history read that cannot be sent.
- **Omnipod Dash** keeps the command queue off the BLE link while the pod is activated.
- **Tidepool** upload notes, deviceId on every record, batches of 1000, real client headers, and
  its own connection settings. A shared `SyncLogEntry` log for Nightscout, xDrip and Tidepool
  replaces `NSClientLog`.
- **Accessibility** across the app: named graph edit controls, switches and sliders that read as
  one control, dialog headings, announcement of control state.
- **Pumps** no longer write passwords and pairing keys to the debug log. Patch pumps do not prime
  from AAPS and have no battery-age or prime settings.

## What arrived from mtr

- **Staging auto-promotion** (`StagingAutoPromotion`). A pre-soak sensor (ONE+ / G7 or Libre 3)
  can switch to production by itself after N hours, when its recent readings are good. Shared
  decision class, per-plugin `AutoPromote` boolean and `AutoPromoteHours` int key, and
  `NotificationId.CGM_STAGING_AUTO_PROMOTED`.
- **OnePlus calibration queue** improvements.
- mtr `appVersion` suffix (`4.0.0.0-dev.AIMI.111026`) **not taken** — this branch keeps
  `4.0.0.0-dev.RB.120926`.
